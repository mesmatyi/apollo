## 1.1. Prerequisites

Please contact the business department (email develop-kit@apollo.auto) to obtain authorization

## 1.2. Register Baidu Cloud BOS

Register for Baidu Cloud BOS by following the [Baidu Cloud Object Storage (BOS) Registration and Basic Usage Guide](./apply_bos_account_cn.md)

## 1.3. Activate the cloud service account
Open the [Dreamland website](http://bce.apollo.auto/) and choose to log in with a Baidu account. After logging in, click the "Fuel User Guide" menu item under "User Help" in the left menu bar, as shown in the figure below, and follow the document to activate the cloud service account.
