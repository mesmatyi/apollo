## Overview

You can use the planning module by publishing commands to it. When your scenario requires multiple planning tasks to complete one job, or you need to dynamically change the task state while a planning task is executing,
you can orchestrate planning tasks yourself in the business layer according to your business needs and publish commands to the planning module.

Commands are sent by the user's business layer in a server-client manner. For details, refer to the document ["External Interface Usage Guide"](../附录/外部接口使用指南.md).

You can also create new customized commands based on your scenario and send them to the planning module, but Apollo's planning module will not respond to them. You can create new scenarios, stages and tasks to handle your customized commands through the secondary development of the planning module described in the next chapter.
