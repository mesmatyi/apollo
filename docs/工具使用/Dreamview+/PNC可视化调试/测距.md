## Overview

Measures the distance between two points, or the total length of a polyline made up of multiple points. It can be used to determine the length of a stretch of road, calculate the distance between objects, determine the distance between two locations, and so on.

## Step 1: Install and Start Dreamview+

See the [Package Management Installation](../安装指南/包管理安装方式.md) document.

## Step 2: Select the Simulation Scenario

In **Operations**, select **Sim_Control** or **Scenario_Sim**. This section uses **Sim_Control** as an example.

## Step 3: Select the Map and Vehicle

1. In **Environment Resources**, select a map. This section uses **San Mateo** as an example.

2. In **ADS Resources**, select a vehicle. This section uses **Mkz Example** as an example.

   > Note: Selecting a vehicle is optional.

3. In the **Vehicle Visualization** panel, click **Routing Editing** to enter the route editing page.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_alpha_doc/image_b3bef25.png)

   > Note: You can also skip opening Routing Editing and directly click the distance measurement button at the lower right of the **Vehicle Visualization** panel.

4. In the **Vehicle Visualization** panel, click the distance measurement button ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_alpha_doc/image_95d8c03.png).

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_alpha_doc/image_4ee20d6.png)

5. In the editing interface, click a series of points, and double-click or right-click on the last point to finish. After the points are selected, next to each point (except the first one) the distance and angle relative to the previous point are displayed.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_alpha_doc/image_352b5bb.png)

   Next to the last point there is an ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_alpha_doc/image_556beae.png) icon, which clears the sequence of coordinate points drawn this time.
