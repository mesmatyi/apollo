## Overview

LiDAR is one of the main sensors of an autonomous driving platform and plays an important role in perception and localization. Because LiDAR installation positions differ, the transformation between the LiDAR coordinate system and the vehicle coordinate system must be determined before use. In Apollo, the method of obtaining this transformation is called calibration, and calibration includes extrinsic calibration and intrinsic calibration. When purchasing a LiDAR, the manufacturer provides accurate LiDAR intrinsics, so this feature mainly implements the LiDAR extrinsic calibration method.

## Module Startup Dependencies

The calibration tool depends on Localization and the gnss, lidar and camera drivers. Before calibration, please make sure the related dependencies are correctly installed and configured.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Select the Mode

On the Dreamview+ page, select the **Lidar Calibration** mode.

![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_9933735.png)


### Step 2: Environment Check

Check the current collection vehicle environment. As shown in the figure above, when the localization status, GPS status and Lidar status are shown in green, the environment check has passed. Click Next.

### Step 3: Confirm Initial Values
1. Confirm the following initial values of each Lidar:
2. After confirming they are correct, click **Confirm**.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_5ffb5d0.png)

   > Note: The error of the initial extrinsic angles must not exceed plus or minus 20 degrees, and the displacement error must not exceed plus or minus 0.5 m.


### Step 4: Dynamic Collection

1. Click **Start Collection**.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_1d37778.png)

2. Drive in circles at 10 km/h in an outdoor site until the progress bar is full. (For the speed, it is recommended to collect at the speed suited to your own vehicle model. During collection, it completes once the progress bar reaches 100%, with no manual intervention required.)

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_61c0ba2.png)

   > Note: A good localization signal is required during collection, and the natural environment should contain walls or other planar objects as environmental features. It is best that there are no dynamically moving objects while collecting data.


### Step 5: Update the Calibration Result

After collection is complete, files are generated automatically.

![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_018da0b.png)

After the files are generated, click **Finish Calibration**.

![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_db91623.png)


Click **Confirm**, and the new calibration parameters overwrite the parameters that already exist on the vehicle.

![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_ce7663e.png)
