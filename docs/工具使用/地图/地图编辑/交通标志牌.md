## Create a Traffic Sign

1. Select a lane.

2. Click **Sign**.

3. In the properties on the right, select the sign type.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_aff193b.png)

4. Click the lane to create the start point of the stop line, and click on the other side of the lane to create the end point of the stop line.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_a80499e.png)

## Move a Traffic Sign

1. Select the traffic sign you want to move.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_3bdeb3e.png)

2. Press and hold the left mouse button and drag to move the traffic sign.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_524785c.png)

## View Traffic Sign Information

Click to select a traffic sign on the map, and the information bar on the right displays its properties.

![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_5e4f64e.png)

## Delete a Traffic Sign

1. Click to select the traffic sign on the lane.

2. Press the **Delete** key to delete the traffic sign.
