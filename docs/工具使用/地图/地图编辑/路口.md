## Draw an Intersection

1. Click the **Intersection** button at the top.

2. Click multiple times on the base map to draw the shape of the intersection.   
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_ca114d1.png)

3. Press **Esc** or **Enter**, or double-click the mouse button, to exit intersection drawing. The intersection is complete.

4. Click to select the intersection and set its properties through **Properties**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_38496c7.png)

## Delete an Intersection

1. Click to select the intersection you want to delete.

2. Press Delete to delete the intersection.
   
   - On Windows, press the **Delete** key.
   - On MAC OS, press **fn** + **Delete**.

## Rotate an Intersection

1. Select the intersection you want to rotate.
   
   After selection, the intersection turns blue.

2. Click the rotate button, and rotation icons appear on both sides of the intersection.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_c775ece.png)
   
   Click the rotation icon to rotate the intersection.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_a097904.png)

3. Click the rotation icon and drag the intersection to finish rotating it.
