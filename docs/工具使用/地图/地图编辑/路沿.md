## Add a Curb

1. Click the **Curb** button.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_e6c41ab.png)

2. Click one side of the selected lane to create the start point of the curb, and click the other side of the selected lane to create the end point of the curb.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_f2224d1.png)
   
   ## Delete a Curb

3. Click to select the curb you want to delete.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_e3839ac.png)
   
   After selection, the curb turns blue.

4. Press the **Delete** key to delete the curb.
   
   - On Windows, press the **Delete** key.
   - On MAC OS, press **fn** + **Delete**.
