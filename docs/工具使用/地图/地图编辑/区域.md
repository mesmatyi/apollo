## Add an Area

### Add a Quadrilateral Area

1. Click the **Area** button.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_0e3cdd6.png)

2. Click the selected area and draw points one by one in order, with adjacent points connected by lines. Double-click, or press the Esc key or the Enter key to finish drawing; the last point is automatically connected to the first point.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_4cf5ee6.png)
   
   > Note: Please draw at least three points.
   
   ### Add a Polygon Area

3. Click the **Area** button.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_0e3cdd6.png)

4. Click the selected area and draw points one by one in order, with adjacent points connected by lines. Double-click, or press the Esc key or the Enter key to finish drawing; the last point is automatically connected to the first point.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_7234ce1.png)
   
   > Note: Please draw at least three points.

## Change Area Properties

1. Click to select the lane whose properties you want to modify.

2. In **Properties** on the right, edit the area properties you want to modify.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_c5bfb78.png)
   
   ## Delete an Area

3. Click to select the area you want to delete.
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_03c1e24.png)
   
   After selection, the area turns blue.

4. Press the **Delete** key to delete the area.
   
   - On Windows, press the **Delete** key.
   - On MAC OS, press **fn** + **Delete**.
