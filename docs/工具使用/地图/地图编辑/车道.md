## Draw a Lane

1. Click the **Lane** button.

2. On the base map, click the first point and the second point to determine the width of the lane, and click the third point to determine the lane length.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_2ceb895.png)
   
   > Note: Multiple clicks are supported to draw multi-segment lanes.

3. In **Properties** on the right, set the properties of the lane you drew.

4. Press **Esc** or **Enter**, or double-click the mouse button, to exit lane drawing.

## Adjust Lane Width

1. Click the lane line you want to adjust.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_5c40f94.png)
   
   After the lane line is selected, it turns blue.

2. Drag the lane line to widen or narrow the whole lane.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_42418c5.png)

## Move a Lane

1. Click to select the lane you want to move.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_958a383.png)
   
   After selection, the lane turns blue.

2. Drag the lane with the mouse to move it.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_77f7c53.png)

## Change Lane Properties

1. Click to select the lane whose properties you want to modify.

2. In **Properties** on the right, edit the lane properties you want to modify.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_ae05c18.png)

## Add a Lane

1. Select a lane.

2. Click the **Add Lane** button, and icons for adding a new lane appear on both sides of the lane line.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_de34011.png)

3. Click the add-lane icons on either side of the lane line to add lanes on either side of the lane as needed.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_07c7c28.png)

## Delete a Lane

1. Select the lane you want to delete.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_82f1d22.png)
   
   After selection, the lane turns blue.

2. Press the Delete key to delete the lane.
   
   - On Windows, press the **Delete** key.
   - On MAC OS, press **fn** + **Delete**.

## Rotate a Lane

1. Select the lane you want to rotate and click the rotate button at the top.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_c72177a.png)
   
   After clicking the rotate button, rotation icons appear on both sides of the lane.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_6c6b6a8.png)

2. Click and drag the lane to rotate it.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_0456443.png)

## Connect and Merge Lanes

1. Hold the **Shift** key to select two or more lanes you want to connect at the same time.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_d5299de.png)

2. Click **Connect** or **Merge** to connect or merge the selected lanes.
   
   - Straight connection
     
     ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_ec8a4e7.png)
   
   - Merge
     
     ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_38345c0.png)
     
     ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_6e96e8e.png)
   
   > Notes:
   > 
   > - Straight connection: connects two lane segments with different properties. After the lanes are connected, their road properties can be changed separately.
   > - Merge: merges two lane segments with the same properties into one lane. After the lanes are merged, the properties can be modified or the lane moved as a whole.

## Split a Lane

### Split Along the Lane Direction

From the driver's perspective, splitting along the lane direction is a longitudinal split, parallel to the lane direction.

1. Select the lane you want to split.

2. Click the **Split Along Lane Direction** button.
   
   The lane is split evenly, and the dividing line is parallel to the lane direction.

### Split Perpendicular to the Lane Direction

From the driver's perspective, splitting perpendicular to the lane direction is a lateral split, perpendicular to the lane direction.

1. Select the lane you want to split.

2. Click the **Split Perpendicular to Lane Direction** button.

3. Click the position where you want to split the lane.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_54e781a.png)
   
   After you click the position where you want to split the lane, the lane is split into two lanes.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_25c7980.png)
