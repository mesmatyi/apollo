## Create a Traffic Light

1. Click the **Traffic Light** button.

2. Click the base map to create the start point, and click again to create the end point, creating a stop line.
   
   After the stop line is created, a traffic light is created along the lane direction at a certain distance from the stop line (default length 7 m), with the traffic light aligned with the midpoint of the stop line.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_f5d3d84.png)

## Move a Traffic Light

1. Select the traffic light.

2. Press and hold the left mouse button and drag to move the traffic light (the stop line does not move).
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_4328ec9.png)

## View Traffic Light Information

1. Click to select the traffic light.

2. The information bar on the right displays the traffic light properties.
   
   You can modify the traffic light properties as needed.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_e6aceeb.png)

## Delete a Traffic Light

1. Click to select the traffic light.

2. Press the Delete key to delete the traffic light.
   
   - On Windows, press the **Delete** key.
   - On MAC OS, press **fn** + **Delete**.
   
   After the traffic light is deleted, the associated stop line is deleted as well.
