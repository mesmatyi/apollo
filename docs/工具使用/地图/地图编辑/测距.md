## Overview

Measures the distance between two points, or the total length of a polyline made up of multiple points. It can be used to determine the length and width of a road, the length of a sidewalk, and so on.

## Procedure

1. Click the **Measure Distance** button.

2. Click a series of points on the map.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_a6c7011.png)
   
   The distance measurement tool can display the length of the segment between two points, the angle formed by segments between points, and the total length obtained by adding up all segment lengths. An X button is displayed next to the last point, which clears the sequence of coordinate points drawn this time.
