## Create a Crosswalk

1. Click the **Crosswalk** button.
2. On the base map, click the first point and the second point to determine the width of the crosswalk, click a third time to determine the length of the crosswalk, and double-click to finish and complete the crosswalk.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_f9ad50a.png)

   > Notes:
   > - The line connecting the first and second points determines what the crosswalk's white stripes are parallel to, and the third point determines the length of the white stripe fence of the crosswalk.
   > - The width of each white stripe of the crosswalk is fixed at 40 cm (as required by regulations).

## Move a Crosswalk

1. Click the crosswalk you want to move.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_9619ca5.png)

   After selection, the crosswalk turns blue.

2. Click, hold and drag to move the crosswalk.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_ba0ecd1.png)

## Rotate a Crosswalk

1. Select the crosswalk you want to rotate.
2. Click the rotate button, and rotation icons appear on both sides of the button.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_67cadeb.png)

3. Move the rotation icon to finish rotating the crosswalk.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_0304bff.png)

## Adjust the Width/Length of a Crosswalk

1. Select the crosswalk, and the boundary points of the crosswalk are highlighted.

   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_70d715f.png)

2. Select and drag the boundary points of the crosswalk to adjust its width and length.

   - Adjust the width

     ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_44a5e61.png)

   - Adjust the length

     ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_2f2994d.png)

## Delete a Crosswalk

1. Select the crosswalk you want to delete.
2. Press the Delete key to delete the crosswalk.

   - On Windows, press the **Delete** key.
   - On MAC OS, press **fn** + **Delete**.
