## Create a Straight Line

1. Click the **Line** button.

2. On the map, click the 1st point to determine the start position.

3. On the map, click the 2nd point, which is connected to the previous point by a straight line to form a line segment.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_54c6750.png)
   
   You can create as many points as needed and connect them into line segments.

4. Click the Nth point, which is connected to the previous point by a straight line to form a line segment.

## Extend a Line

1. Select the line you want to extend, and extension plus buttons <img src="https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_003284e.png" width = "20" height = "20"/> appear at both endpoints of the line.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_38d98b2.png)

2. Click the extend button at one endpoint, then click on the map to determine the end point of the extension. This point is connected to the line endpoint by a straight line.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_917ba89.png)
   
   > Note: After clicking the extend button on one side, the extend buttons at both endpoints disappear.

## Connect Two Lines

1. Select the line you want to connect, and extension plus buttons appear at both endpoints along the line direction.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_385a76f.png)

2. Click the extension at one endpoint, then click an endpoint of the second line. This point is connected to the lane endpoint by a straight line.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_825c4af.png)

## Move a Line

1. Select the line you want to move, and the line is highlighted.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_1ef7849.png)

2. Press and hold the left mouse button again and drag to move the element.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_c39cc40.png)
   
   > Note: If the line forms a lane or an area, the lane or area type does not change after the move.
