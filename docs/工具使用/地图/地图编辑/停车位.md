## Create a Parking Space

1. Click the **Parking Space** button.

2. On the base map, click the first point and the second point to determine the width of the parking space, and click the third point to determine its length.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_5ce34e6.png)

## Copy a Parking Space

1. Select a parking space, and the **Add Parking Space** button appears in the operation area at the upper right of the view.

2. Click the **Add Parking Space** button, and **+** buttons appear around the parking space.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_d046cee.png)

3. After clicking, a parking space of the same size is copied to the adjacent position. After a successful copy, the last newly added parking space is selected, with **+** buttons displayed around it.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_3c8c868.png)

## Modify the Shape of a Parking Space

1. Select the parking space you want to modify.

2. Modify it through the property input boxes on the right.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_d289465.png)

3. Modify the properties on the right and view the modified parking space.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_455d612.png)

## Rotate a Parking Space

1. Select the parking space you want to rotate.
   
   After selection, the parking space turns blue.

2. Click the **Rotate** button.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_63af776.png)

3. After clicking the rotate button, rotation icons appear around the parking space, with the center of the space as the center point.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_de03980.png)

4. Move any icon to rotate the parking space.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Beta_Doc/image_e59155f.png)

## Delete a Parking Space

1. Select the parking space you want to delete.

2. Press the Delete key to delete the parking space.
   
   - On Windows, press the **Delete** key.
   - On MAC OS, press **fn** + **Delete**.
