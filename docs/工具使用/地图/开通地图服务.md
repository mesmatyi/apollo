## Activate Map Editing

The map editing feature is a free service. You can apply for a free trial as an individual, or try the map editing feature for free as an organization.

- Individual free trial: map editing (map collection and the editor's base map import are not supported; only drawing simulation maps is supported)
- Organization free trial: map collection and map editing

### Apply for Map Editing with an Individual Account

1. Log in to the [Apollo Developer Community](https://apollo.baidu.com/).

2. In the menu bar, select **Products** > **Development Tools** > **Map and Calibration**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_00474f7.png)

3. Click **Free Trial** under Map Editing.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_addb524.png)
   
   It shows that the application for the map editing feature succeeded.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_8c23076.png)

### Apply for Map Editing with an Organization Account

1. Log in to the [Apollo Developer Community](https://apollo.baidu.com/).

2. In the menu bar, select **Products** > **Development Tools** > **Map and Calibration**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_00474f7.png)

3. Select the workspace identity and click **Confirm**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_6419eb4.png)

4. On the **Map and Calibration** application page, click **Free Trial**.

5. On the **Trial Application** page, edit the tool type and other related information of the application and click **OK**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_40ca5bc.png)
   
   It shows that the application succeeded.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_99f11ed.png)

6. Click your profile picture > **Organization Services**, and select **Service Benefits**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_451bc1a.png)
   
   It shows that the map editing feature has been activated.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_9f15406.png)
   
   > Note: The map editing free trial is activated as soon as you apply, with no approval required.

## Activate Map Collection

This section uses an activation application as an example to describe how to apply for the map collection service. You can also submit a free trial application and use the service after approval.

> Note: An activation application is for customers who have purchased map collection, and is submitted based on the purchase contract number and service content. It is currently open to organization accounts.

### Step 1: Apply to Activate the Service

1. Log in to the [Apollo Developer Community](https://apollo.baidu.com/).

2. In the menu bar, select **Products** > **Development Tools** > **Map and Calibration**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_4d9ed43.png)

3. Select the workspace identity and click **Confirm**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_6419eb4.png)

4. On the **Map and Calibration** application page, click **Activation Application**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_f4745e5.png)
   
   > Note: The map collection feature requires an application. If you choose the free trial or an activation application, you must wait for approval by the relevant personnel before you can use it.

5. On the **Activation Application** page, edit the tool type and other related information of the application and click **OK**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_c079d66.png)
   
   It shows that the map service application succeeded.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_acfff98.png)
   
   After applying, it shows that the service you applied for is under approval; please wait patiently for approval. After approval is granted, return to Configuration Management to complete the tool activation described below.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_271b591.png)

### Step 2: Activate the Service

1. In Personal Workspace > Configuration Management, select the **Map Collection** tab.

2. Click **Activate**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_a507166.png)

3. Click **Confirm**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_d4690a2.png)
   
   It shows that the map was activated successfully.
   
   In **Service Benefits**, you can see that the map service you applied for has been activated.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_6a7b2be.png)

### Step 3: Add a Map Area

1. Click **Add Area**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_365eba2.png)

2. Click the area you want to add on the map, edit the area name, and click **OK**.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_f1abe7c.png)
   
   You can see that the area was added successfully.
   
   ![image.png](https://bce.bdstatic.com/doc/Apollo-Homepage-Document/Apollo_Studio/image_5372863.png)
   
   > Note: After a map area is added, it cannot be deleted or have its position changed, so please confirm the area before adding it.
