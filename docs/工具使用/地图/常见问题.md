### 2. What should I do if login fails or plugin sync fails?

* First check whether the device has a network connection, because the plugin connection requires a network.
* If the network is connected and you are connecting to the device remotely over SSH, failures may occur occasionally due to tunnel stability issues. Try a few more times, or regenerate the plugin command and retry.

## Map Collection

### 1. Why is the Lidar/GNSS/Localization hardware status red?

Please check whether the hardware connection is normal. You can open cyber monitor to check the data flow of the following key channels:

* GNSS-related: `/apollo/sensor/gnss/best_pose`, `/apollo/sensor/gnss/odometry`,
* LiDAR-related: usually those ending in PointCloud2, for example `/apollo/sensor/lidar16/compensator/PointCloud2`,
* Localization-related: `/apollo/localization/pose`; the status in `/apollo/localization/msf_status` must not be ERROR (WARNING is fine).

### 2. What is the difference between Common mode and Slam mode, and which one should I use?

* The mapping algorithms are different: Common mainly relies on RTK, while Slam, as the name suggests, relies on a SLAM algorithm.
* If the site is open and has no overhead obstruction (outdoors), you can choose Common mode.
* If there are many objects around the site, or there is overhead obstruction and the GNSS signal is unstable, you can choose Slam mode.

## Map Editing

### 1. Importing a base map file shows no permission. How do I get permission?

Contact the Apollo team to enable trial/usage permission for your account, and the import will then succeed.

### 2. How do I adjust the curvature of a curve?

Click to select the edge line of the curve, and lever handles appear at the endpoints. Click and drag a handle to adjust the curvature.

### 3. How do I draw a crossroad? What does the Junction box mean?

* First draw the lanes in each direction with straight lanes, then select the corresponding roads in pairs and choose curve connection in the upper right corner.
* The Junction box is the intersection semantics in the HD map; it marks this area as an intersection.
  
  ### 4. How do I check that my elements are usable by the perception and PnC algorithms?
  * In the curve properties at turns, the direction has been specified as left turn/right turn (modify it manually if it has not been changed).
  * Lanes that have been through "Split Along Lane Direction" already have connection relationships with their predecessors and successors (if there is no connection, you can hold shift to select the two lanes and click straight connection in the upper right corner to establish the connection relationship).
  * The middle line at places where lane changes are allowed is a dashed line.
